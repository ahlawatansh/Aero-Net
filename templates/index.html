About AeroNet

AeroNet is an embedded edge-AI surveillance and airspace defense system developed as a Final Year Capstone Project for industry placements. Designed to protect sensitive airspace, high-security zones, and critical infrastructure, AeroNet captures real-time live camera streams, performs low-latency target localization via YOLOv8, extracts deep visual features with ResNet-18, and classifies targets in real time with high precision.

The system demonstrates an end-to-end, edge-deployable embedded surveillance architecture engineered for real-world defense, low-altitude perimeter monitoring, and facility security operations.
