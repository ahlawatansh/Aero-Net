About AeroNet

AeroNet is an autonomous edge-AI airspace defense surveillance system engineered specifically for military defense perimeters, no-fly zones, and drone-restricted airspace. Built to safeguard sensitive installations, borders, and critical national infrastructure, AeroNet monitors incoming aerial vectors in real time, isolates airborne signatures with YOLOv8, and performs deep neural feature classification with ResNet-18 to instantly discriminate between benign biological targets (birds) and unauthorized unmanned aerial systems (UAVs / drones).

When an unauthorized drone breaches a protected restricted zone, the system initiates immediate threat tracking, alerts surveillance operators, and delivers actionable aerial telemetry for anti-drone countermeasure interdiction.
